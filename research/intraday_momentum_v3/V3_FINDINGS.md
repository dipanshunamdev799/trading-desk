## Supported findings

1. **The snapshot API works.** Full market quotes succeed with the configured read-only token. Local token metadata is unexpired. The streaming authorization failure is specifically HTTP 401 `UDAPI100058`, before any subscription, with an inactive-account-segments message. Official Analytics Token documentation supports WebSockets, so a blanket claim that a static/read-only token cannot stream is incorrect. The broker's account diagnosis needs account-owner/support clarification; no token rotation or account change was made.
2. **The archive is larger than the earlier RELIANCE sample.** Complete row/time inventory covers 32,370,042 updates, 1,050 instruments and eight sessions. Cash equity contributes 5,506,783 rows across 50 stocks. Forty-seven are from the V2 liquid core and only three are outside it. This archive is not broad coverage of volatile smaller stocks.
3. **Quote continuity is a material limitation.** In the prespecified eight-stock/16-day detailed subset, fresh valid one-second coverage is 65.48-97.72%, median 82.49%; maximum interarrival gap exceeds 37 minutes. There are zero exact source/payload duplicates in those 348,484 inspected updates. Depth/type counts across the larger archive are explicitly sampled, not universal claims.
4. **The apparent MIS/suspension contradiction had a join error.** ISIN keys occur in different trading series. Exact exchange/segment/type/token/key matching finds zero conflicts among current ordinary EQ identities; 1,372 of 2,763 are MIS-listed. This resolves current-listing identity, not historical eligibility or account permission. HFCL is currently BE and was excluded from ordinary EQ observation.
5. **Quoted capacity matters as much as spread.** For HDFCBANK on 13 November, an approximately Rs100,000 immediate round trip has a conditional median cost of 10.48 bps under 10%-of-displayed-depth bounds and estimated fees, with adequate depth at 96.89% of sampled points. Adequate modeled depth falls to 22.26% around Rs500,000 and 1.47% around Rs1 million. These are bounds, not fills. Subsecond execution prices are often unidentifiable at the archive's cadence.
6. **A broader prospective observer is implemented.** The current plan has 479 ordinary-EQ stocks, 20 fixed matched controls, bounded dynamic rich-depth rotation and NIFTY/VIX context. Authentic Upstox payloads append to existing PostgreSQL raw tables; derived research stays separate. No new raw-data source, database schema, application bot setting or global switch was introduced.
7. **Exactly three prospective hypotheses are specified.** Persistent momentum support, liquidity-confirmed breakout and exhaustion/failed continuation. P/PV/PVD baselines share events and use separate training/evaluation sessions. Missing features, stale quotes, partial depth, unverified shorts and unproven economic edge reject hypothetical participation.
