# Funded-account diagnostic, fixed before execution

No prior artifacts or strategies are modified. Three diagnostic reference rules
are the already-researched frozen KAMA-cross/RS, EMA-cross/RS and hybrid ribbon
pullback/breadth. Their prior ranking contaminates this entire retrospective
comparison; they are not newly selected on an untouched holdout.

Each rule separately trades one Rs1,000,000 cash account, long-only, next open,
maximum ten concurrent positions. Simultaneous candidates rank descending by
completed 60-session relative strength vs NIFTY, with symbol alphabetical ties.
The allocation comparison is fixed: equal-weight 10% equity; volatility-weighted
10%*(20% / annualized prior60 daily volatility), capped20%; risk-based0.5% equity
at planned stop, capped20%. All allocations respect actual cash including fees,
prior20 volume1% participation and Rs5crore turnover. Sector cap30% of prior
marked account equity. Add one correlation ablation to risk sizing (reject a
candidate if its past60 return correlation with a held stock exceeds0.8, at least
40 finite observations); add one drawdown ablation to that policy (suspend new
entries for the rest of a segment once observed close-to-close drawdown reaches
15%, while continuing protective exits). Thresholds are diagnostic references,
not optimized recommendations.

Retain original rule exits/stops/hold limits. Same-day stop before target and
adverse gap conventions remain. Fees initially reproduce the original delivery
model for comparability; audit reports separately assess classification errors.
Economic dividend credit on ex-date, no cash interest, no leverage, no tax on
income/capital gains. Historical spread/volume assumptions remain uncertified.
Only completed prior data enter ranking/correlation/sizing. Use today's known
open for executable sizing; held exposure is valued at prior close translated
into current share units. Corporate action entitlements are applied before
exits. Entry cost and principal are reserved as real cash, unlike independent
sleeve aggregation. Stop/target exits release cash for other stocks that day;
stocks held at today's start cannot reenter that same session.

Report all five policies for all three rules, chronological development,
validation, final and full two-year context. One prespecified higher-cost
scenario (double fees/10bps) for risk+correlation. Each segment starts cash, while
full-period paths carry positions across internal split dates. This is an
accounting/sizing diagnostic, not a new profitability search. Include equity,
trades, utilization, turnover, rejections and allocation invariant checks.
