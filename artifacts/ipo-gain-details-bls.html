About Company

Sector - Plastic Products - Industrial
Incorporated1991

BLS Polymers specializes in the development and manufacturing of customized polymer compounds for essential infrastructure industries, including telecommunications, power, railways, water management, and oil and gas distribution.

Product Portfolio: The company offers a broad range of polymer-based materials, including high-density polyethylene (HDPE), medium-density polyethylene (MDPE), linear low-density polyethylene (LLDPE), cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE), halogen-free flame-retardant (HFFR) compounds, polyvinyl chloride (PVC), cable filling compounds (CFC), and adhesive coating compounds.

Domestic Reach and Customer Base: BLS Polymers has developed an extensive distribution network spanning 24 Indian states and union territories. During Fiscal 2025, the company catered to 113 repeat customers and added 53 new customers to its portfolio.

Geographical Market Presence: The company serves both domestic and international markets, exporting its products to countries such as Bangladesh, Egypt, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, the United Arab Emirates, the United States of America, and Zambia. In Fiscal 2025, domestic sales accounted for 91.57% of total revenue, while exports contributed the remaining 8.43%.

Manufacturing Infrastructure: BLS Polymers operates a manufacturing facility in Goa covering approximately 24,220 square feet. The facility is equipped with advanced machinery to support its polymer compound manufacturing operations.

Competitive Advantages

  • Approved supplier status with established government agencies, supported by a portfolio of pre-approved products.
  • A diversified product range serving multiple industries and infrastructure applications.
  • Established customer relationships built through long-term business associations.
  • A broad pan-India distribution network covering 24 states and union territories.
  • Integrated product development capabilities that support customized polymer solutions.
