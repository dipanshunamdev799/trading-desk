Core4 Engineers IPO Details


About Core4 Engineers

Founded in 2022, Core4 Engineers is an EPCC company that offers integrated infrastructure solutions for railway infrastructure, power transmission & distribution, and water & wastewater treatment sectors. The company undertakes end-to-end project execution, from design, engineering, procurement, erection, testing, commissioning, and operation & maintenance (O&M). 

Its business verticals include railway electrification, railway infrastructure, power transmission and distribution, water and wastewater treatment, automation and SCADA systems, and HT/LT electrical panel manufacturing. The company undertakes infrastructure projects for Indian Railways, government-owned companies (PSUs), and other infrastructure contractors. Core4 Engineers operates its own in-house manufacturing facility for High-Tension (HT) and Low-Tension (LT) electrical panels and compact substations.
