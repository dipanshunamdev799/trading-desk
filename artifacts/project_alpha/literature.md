# Project Alpha: primary evidence and falsifiable mechanisms

Sources checked9November2026. External evidence motivates tests; it does not establish current NIFTY option-buying profitability. Historical samples, markets, information sets and execution assumptions matter. Findings below are paraphrased; no social-media profit claim is treated as audited evidence.

## Price formation and liquidity

**Intraday momentum.** Gao, Han, Li and Zhou's2018 study documents first-to-last-half-hour predictability in historical US ETF returns, with state dependence. Mechanism candidates are delayed information absorption and infrequent rebalancing. Test a predeclared continuation rule against matched-time momentum/random directions, by volatility and time of day; falsify if incremental net returns fail later periods or disappear after spread/latency. A NIFTY afternoon EMA rule is related but is not a replication of their specification. [Journal paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X18301351), [author university record](https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/market-intraday-momentum/).

**Indian intraday liquidity.** Krishnan and Mishra2013 study trades and quotes for NIFTY constituent stocks and report intraday variation in liquidity, including concurrent high volume and wide spreads. This supports measuring the cost curve rather than assuming the busiest opening period is cheapest. Re-estimate clock-bucket return variance and spreads using dated data; classify regimes using information available then. Falsify an opening-breakout opportunity if its movement advantage is consumed by entry spreads and missed fills. This is equity-stock liquidity evidence, not a NIFTY option-premium result. [Monash primary publication record](https://research.monash.edu/en/publications/intraday-liquidity-patterns-in-indian-stock-market/).

**Futures/spot price discovery.** Raju and Karande'sSEBI-hosted2003 working paper studies the introduction of NIFTY futures and price discovery/volatility. It motivates synchronized futures/spot tests but comes from a very different market. Cointegration or a lead-lag statistic is not an executable forecast after latency and spread. Use exact dated futures, carry/roll/basis controls and contemporaneous timestamps. Reject the trading claim if information is incorporated before a feasible order or the result depends on stale spot observations. [SEBI working paper](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/1293096997650.pdf).

**Auction changes are real structural breaks.** NSE confirms CAS live from3August2026; its current schedule distinguishes cash-stock auction behavior from derivatives trading. An index may display a closing-price jump when underlying auctions resolve. Keep raw prices and separate session phases; do not treat auction prices as continuous-session tradable option fills. [Implementation circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR75479.pdf), [current CAS schedule](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/closing-auction-session).

## Why an option buyer needs more than direction

For a small move, an explanatory approximation is:

`premium change ≈ delta × spot change + ½ gamma × spot change² + vega × IV change + theta × elapsed time`.

This is a local attribution approximation, not a fill model or a guaranteed return. Parameters change as price, expiry and volatility change; jumps and spreads matter. Delta defines directional sensitivity, gamma curvature, theta time sensitivity and vega volatility sensitivity. Near expiry, nonlinear repricing makes fixed-delta proxies particularly unreliable. [OIC Greeks](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/understanding-options-greeks), [theta](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/theta), [vega](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/vega).

**Variance risk premium.** Bakshi and Kapadia2003 find negative average delta-hedged gains in historical S&P500 options, consistent with compensation for volatility risk. This is evidence that purchased convexity can carry a cost; it does not imply every directional long-option trade loses. A sufficiently strong, fast conditional directional move can offset that drag. Measure actual contract P&L, compare expiry/delta choices and separate delta exposure from volatility/time effects when contemporaneous Greeks exist. Falsify a convexity advantage if a matched directional instrument or conservative quote replay dominates after costs. [Author's published-paper copy](https://people.umass.edu/~nkapadia/docs/Bakshi_and_Kapadia_2003_RFS.pdf).

**Indian VIX is context, not a free direction forecast.** Thenmozhi and Chandra's2013 NSE working paper reports an asymmetric relationship between NIFTY returns and changes in India VIX. That motivates conditional volatility tests, not mechanically buying calls when VIX falls. A contemporaneous correlation can be a response to the same price movement. Evaluate VIX inputs known before the entry and test incremental contribution against price/volatility baselines. Phase-two low/not-rising-VIX filters did not establish an edge in this project. [NSE working paper](https://archives.nseindia.com/research/content/res_WorkingPaper9.pdf).

**Additional India-specific leads.** Varma's2002 IIMA paper studies volatility pricing in the early Indian index-option market. It is useful historical context, with substantial maturity/regime differences from2026. Publisher discovery also located Sankar, Ramachandran and Lukose's2020 *Dynamics of variance risk premium: Evidence from India*, but full text was inaccessible in this session; no numerical result from it is adopted. A recent public author'sNIFTY variance study uses daily NSE archives and reports no significant next-month directional predictability. Its code is an exploratory data-acquisition/methodology lead, not independently replicated trading evidence. Its theoretical short-volatility return is outside this long-option mission. [IIMA paper](https://web.iima.ac.in/publications/data/2002-04-01JRVarma.pdf), [2020 publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1059056020301222), [public author's code](https://github.com/youngmoon8/india-variance-risk-premium).

## OI, PCR and information

Pan and Poteshman2006 find informative option volume in US stocks using a distinctive buyer-initiated opening-volume dataset. They specifically distinguish nonpublic opening-flow information from publicly reconstructed signals. Public aggregate OI PCR and total put/call volume do not reproduce that information set. An OI increase contains both a long and a short side; it does not identify naked writers, directional intent or a price barrier. Test lagged same-expiry public measures as incremental context, controlling for price and volatility, with point-in-time publication assumptions stated. Reject an OI/PCR rule if a matched price-only policy does as well or improvements vanish in later sessions. [Author's published-paper copy](https://web.mit.edu/junpan/www/volume.pdf).

Prior-day NSE EOD positioning is a valid coarse hypothesis when available by the next decision; intraday OI change is a different hypothesis requiring actual earlier captures. Strike concentration may affect liquidity or hedging but cannot be interpreted as measured dealer gamma without positions, signs and hedging assumptions. Max pain is a static intrinsic-payout calculation, not a price-attractor theorem.

## Contract and cost history

Use dated contract records. NIFTY lots changed from25 to75 for newly introduced contracts under theOctober2024 circular; theOctober2025 circular's75-to65 transition applied differently by expiry. TheJune2025 expiry circular preserves earlier expiries and realigns contracts fromSeptember2025. Do not globally apply today'slot/Tuesday expiry to earlier records. [2024 lot circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP64625.pdf), [2025 lot circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP70616.pdf), [expiry transition](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP68747.pdf).

Sale STT changed from0.10% of option premium to0.15% on1April2026. NSE'sMarch2026 fee circular reallocates exchange charges/IPFT while keeping their combined option outflow₹3,553 per crore per side. This avoids double-counting oldIPFT on top of a newly quoted combined rate. Other costs include actual brokerage plan, GST, stamp duty, SEBI levy and slippage. Historical tariff and rounding still need a contract-note reconciliation. [STT circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FATAX73524.pdf), [exchange/IPFT circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FA73061.pdf), [broker's dated rate-card sections](https://upstox.com/brokerage-charges/).

Current Upstox documentation distinguishes regular/registered algo order limits and standard data limits. Read-only data calls and order execution have different permissions and failure semantics. The documented standard limits are50requests/second,500/minute and2,000/30minutes; a script's bounded collection must respect all windows. Market feed full mode supplies five book levels and Greeks, while request counts/connection categories constrain capture. These are present-day documented limits, not proof of historical availability or permission to place orders. [API rate limits](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/rate-limiting/), [V3 feed specification](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-market-data-feed/).

## Research inference and rejection criteria

The plausible long-option opportunity is conditional: directional information and/or underpriced realized movement must overcome premium decay, repricing, spread and fees. Public evidence does not identify a universal indication that does this for every state. A no-trade outcome is required when execution data are missing, costs dominate plausible movement, or the state is outside tested coverage.

Searching many variants creates false discoveries. Chronological selection, overlap-aware validation, a complete experiment ledger, block uncertainty and new data are required; a bootstrap conditional on chosen rules does not erase the search. Monte Carlo reorderings describe sensitivity under stated dependence assumptions; they do not create additional market observations. [Bailey etal., backtest overfitting](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf).

Every hypothesis in `hypotheses.json` states a mechanism, valid information set, comparator and falsifier. The deployment gate remains closed unless the same frozen rule survives realistic premium execution, parameter/cost/latency perturbations, statistically adequate later evidence and feasible capital limits. No paper in this review substitutes for that local evidence.
