# V21 Information Source Specialization

The source comparisons start from the same dated technical and structural information. They introduce filing counts, semantic or financial surprise information, already-observed event reactions, and delivery or transaction activity one block at a time. The matched event-present subset shows whether company effects also emerge near actual releases. A fixed-law test changes only the company locations in the simultaneous stock scenarios, keeping relative dependence and the index-basis atoms. Its signed weights test whether the company improvements reach index contribution forecasts. A separate native weekly mechanism checks the weighted corporate reaction as a residual correction to the archived V20 distribution.
