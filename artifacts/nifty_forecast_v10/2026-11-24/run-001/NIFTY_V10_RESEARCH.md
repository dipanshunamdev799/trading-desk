# NIFTY V10 Research

V10 extends the 5- and 21-session study using only 2025–2026 input history and outcomes. The primary frozen snapshot ends on 17 November 2026. The separately checked market refresh ends on 2026-11-23. All evaluated historical cohorts are research-exposed.

**67,514 scored predictions · 155 registered configurations · 32,860 matched contrasts · 16 research reports**

The weekly training dates mattered at a fixed 85-row budget. The best full-coverage V10 weekly scores were **0.236181** Brier from **J10_STRUCTURAL_BLEND** and **0.010892** CRPS from **J10_HISTORY_SHRINK**. The model family and claims are limited by error overlap and adaptive search exposure. The monthly comparison has only **11** disjoint windows.
