# V10 Final Research Conclusions

V10 scored **67,514 forecasts** in **155 registered configurations**, producing **32,860 matched contrasts** with the same dates and realized endpoints. The experiment reproduced the previously reported later weekly training length sensitivity and showed that **which 85 dates are selected** has a large effect at a fixed budget. The recent 85 and systematically spread 85 yielded later Brier **0.376831** and **0.243061**, respectively. Full-history block aggregation improved compact Ridge from combined weekly Brier **0.275044** to **0.260245**. Neither result overturns the frozen weekly references.

On the full weekly V10 chronology **J10_STRUCTURAL_BLEND** had the lowest new Brier at **0.236181**, with CRPS **0.011043**. **J10_HISTORY_SHRINK** had the lowest new CRPS at **0.010892**, with Brier **0.242366**. For comparison, V9 cluster-balanced rotation was **0.236386** Brier and the V9 reliability blend was **0.011014** CRPS. The frozen V8 later weekly direction Brier was **0.159504**, and its protected distribution CRPS was **0.009453**. Selected improvements are provisional after the search adjustment and earlier/later comparison. The symmetric monthly model remains the strongest low-complexity full-coverage reference, with **0.250000** Brier and **0.025420** CRPS.

Authentic equal-weight breadth, extra intraday observations, date-grouped panels and directly decomposed targets improved coverage of available information. Training geometry reduction and complete probability laws made the forecasting problem easier to audit. Cross-market borrowing, recent-only learning, and stronger dependence correction did not reliably close the probability or distribution gap. A 2025–2026 retrospective run can prioritize a future test but cannot independently validate these choices against new unexplored history.
